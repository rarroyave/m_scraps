Same fixed cells, compositions, initial occupations, targets and metrics across methods. Full enumeration, symmetry reduction and bound-aware stopping are compared with the cell-search Python SA, not native SCRAPS SA. One directed first-shell component per case makes max, RMS and MAE numerically identical. No higher-order correlation claims.

Enumeration is capped before symmetry reduction; skipped rows are explicit, not failed optimizations. SA has three seeds. Times include per-method preparation but exclude interpreter startup and validation. Threshold times are observed improvement callback times; a dash means not observed, not zero or the budget. Each final witness is independently reanalyzed. Geometry is held fixed: this is not a supercell-shape search. Bounds/certificates concern one cell only.

This limited deterministic screen does not establish broad statistical superiority. Protocol, environment, measured records and embedded reference witnesses are archived in tools/fixtures/small_cells/. Threshold comparisons use an absolute tolerance of 1e-12.
