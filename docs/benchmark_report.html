What the archived trials show

At maximum absolute correlation error ≤0.01, cuckoo search reached the threshold in 5 of 10 trials; each other method reached it in 2 of 10. These are descriptive counts for the tested problems and seeds, not estimates of general success probabilities.

Campaign design

Single disordered sublattice
BCC Nb–Mo–Ta, 128 and 432 sites.
Two disordered sublattices
Rocksalt (Ti,Zr,Hf)(C,N), 128 and 432 sites, with separate metal and anion composition constraints.
Targets
Independent-site random correlations and exact correlations of an ordering-biased reference configuration. The reference proves nonzero-target attainability but never initializes a search. Random targets may be unattainable exactly in a finite cell. This is not a sweep over all SRO strengths.
Sampling
Seed 20261019 on every combination. Seed 21261019 repeats only the two 128-site nonzero-target cases: 10 problem/seed combinations × four methods.
Equal resource allocation
30 seconds including startup and initialization. Native methods use four MPI ranks; mcsqs and icet use four independent workers.
Search objective
Matched ATAT matching-range objective, WD=0, WN=1, WR=1, tolerance=0.001. Optimization scores are distinct from actual correlation errors.

Recorded host: Apple M1 Max Mac Studio; 10 physical cores, 64 GiB RAM; macOS-14.3-arm64-arm-64bit-Mach-O. ATAT 3.52 and icet 4.0 were used for the external comparisons. Timing comparisons apply to this environment and allocation.

Definitions and interpretation

Actual correlation errors
For achieved-minus-target residual Δᵢ over K assessed components: maximum absolute error = maxᵢ abs(Δᵢ); RMS = √(Σᵢ Δᵢ²/K); mean absolute error = Σᵢ abs(Δᵢ)/K. All methods use the same component definitions for each case. These tolerances do not imply a calibrated error in energy or another material property.
Lowest observed error
The minimum maximum absolute error among valid structures observed within the budget. RMS, mean absolute, and per-order errors in the tables describe that selected structure; they need not equal the independently lowest values of those metrics.
First-observed time
Readers sample at 1–10, 15, 20, 25, and 29.98 seconds, plus completed native outputs. Native checkpoints are emitted at proposal boundaries at intervals of at least 0.25 seconds. Read-completion timestamps determine availability; observations after 30 seconds are excluded. Threshold times are upper bounds on internal attainment times.
Censoring and limitations
Failure to reach a threshold means right-censoring at 30 seconds, not a measured time-to-solution. Few seeds, two structure families, and finite cell sizes limit generalization. Different machines, budgets, or objective functions cannot support direct speedup claims from these results. Examples for diborides, MAX phases, and Heusler phases are not extra trials in this campaign.

Reproduce and audit

Frozen results and fixtures require neither ATAT nor icet. The first command reads archived data; the second runs a separate short native-method demonstration, not this full campaign.

m_scraps benchmark --report-only --output archived-results
m_scraps benchmark --seconds 5 --ranks 4 --output fresh-results

The full four-method rerun requires the documented external engines. Routine regression tests check saved numerical references, not wall-clock performance.

Regenerate this standalone report from the repository:

python tools/benchmark/submission/html_report.py --output docs/benchmark_report.html
